The six word-shapes

  • State = what exists now.
  • Position = where you stand relative to something.
  • Space = all possibilities.
  • Surface/frontier = trade-offs or limits.
  • Flow = movement through time.
  • Exposure = what can reach and affect you.

How to internalise a term

  • First picture the visceral analogy.
  • Then identify the exact relationship it preserves.
  • Use the term only when it adds precision.
  • Test it against a neighbouring term.
  • Translate it back into plain English after use.
